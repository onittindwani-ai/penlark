# Start here

Penlark helps one person or a small team write posts, scripts, emails and newsletters in their own voice, with a person approving everything before it goes anywhere.

## What it does
- Reads writing you own: markdown, text, PDF, a pasted post, a transcript, a LinkedIn Shares.csv or an X archive file.
- Measures how you write (<!--n:dimensions-->14<!--/n--> style dimensions, worked out by code) and keeps a plain language guide that you edit and lock.
- Turns the calls, comments and emails you got into ranked ideas, each with the exact words people used.
- Writes a skeleton draft in your voice and leaves a [BRACKET] wherever a real fact is needed.
- Scores any text against your voice as you type, and flags banned phrases and claims that have no source.
- Asks a second person to approve. Then it saves a file to copy, plans a day on the calendar, or sends to Postiz, Buffer or Metricool when you switch it on.
- Imports post numbers, shows what your best posts have in common (with sample sizes), tracks which post brought a click or a lead, and runs simple A/B tests.

## What it will not do
- It never invents a personal fact, a number, a client or a result. A claim with no source note is flagged "needs a source or a founder fact".
- It never publishes by itself. Draft mode sends nothing. A send needs live mode, an approved draft and an explicit confirm.
- It reads only text you own or give it. It does not scrape anyone's account.
- It is built for your own voice. It does not copy a named public person.
- The offline writer is a set of templates, not a language model. Its drafts are skeletons.

## Who it is for
A founder, a consultant, a creator or a small marketing team that wants the writing to sound like the person and wants a human gate. An implementer can set up one copy per client (doc 10).
