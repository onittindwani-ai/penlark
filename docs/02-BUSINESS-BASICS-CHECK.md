# Business basics check

Before you use Penlark for a real person or a client, read the shared business basics guide in the hub repository (a short checklist on who owns the content, what permission you need to store someone's writing, and what you promise the client). Then confirm:

- You own the writing you load, or the person gave it to you for this purpose. Voice profiles are for the owner of the writing. Do not load another person's posts to copy their voice.
- Every person whose notes (calls, comments, emails) you load knows their words may be quoted in drafts, or you remove names first.
- Each social network's own rules and app approvals are handled by the scheduler tool you pick, not by Penlark.
- You have agreed who approves. Penlark needs two people: the one who asks and the one who approves.
- You have agreed the banned phrases and the facts you will never claim.

Run `python3 tools/doctor.py` before the first live day. It prints the names of problems and never a secret.
